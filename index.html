Attorney-closing states

We work alongside counsel, not around them

In states where an attorney must conduct or supervise the closing, we partner with the deal's counsel or with approved local attorneys. We handle the title side and underwriting placement. Counsel handles what state law reserves to them.

The result is the same national capacity and responsiveness, delivered in a way that fits each state's closing rules.

How we work

From first call to issued policy

Call us at LOI. The earlier we see the deal, the earlier capacity, curative issues and closing logistics are settled.

Call a principal

Property, structure, parties, liability and timing. A summary is enough to start.
