Principal

Ken Manqueros

Principal of Innovative Title and Settlement Services, with more than 30 years of title insurance leadership.

Kenneth (Ken) Manqueros is a Principal of Innovative Title and Settlement Services, a national commercial title and settlement company focused on high-liability transactions.

Career in title insurance

Ken brings more than 30 years of title insurance leadership, including about two decades at First American in national roles. He served as President of First American's National Agency division, held regional leadership in National Commercial Services, and was National Sales Manager for the lender group.

That career has centered on large, complex transactions and the underwriter relationships behind them. He has built and grown title and settlement platforms, structured multi-party real estate transactions, and partnered with lenders, developers and institutional investors.

At Innovative

Ken leads underwriting strategy and capacity placement for high-liability commercial, development and energy transactions. Innovative represents the market rather than a single underwriter, so each deal goes to the underwriter, or combination of underwriters, best suited to carry it.

He handles transactions directly, from mid-size deals that large national operations often leave waiting to those no single underwriter will carry alone.

Areas of focus

  • High-liability placement through co-insurance and reinsurance
  • Multi-site and multi-state portfolios
  • Energy and infrastructure projects
  • Development, construction and distressed transactions
