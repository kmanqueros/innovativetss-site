Anthony Knight

Principal of Innovative Title and Settlement Services, with more than 30 years of title insurance leadership.

Anthony Knight is a Principal of Innovative Title and Settlement Services, a national commercial title and settlement company focused on high-liability transactions.

Career in title insurance

Anthony brings more than 30 years of title insurance leadership across sales, operations and business development for national and regional title organizations.

He has launched and scaled title operations, built high-performing teams, and developed long-standing relationships with lenders, developers, builders and institutional partners.

At Innovative

Anthony leads client relationships and national licensing, keeping enterprise and development clients covered wherever their transactions take them. Innovative represents the market rather than a single underwriter, so each deal goes to the underwriter, or combination of underwriters, best suited to carry it.
